UK Careers Framework

Mapped to the Gatsby Benchmarks

In England, schools and colleges are required to provide career guidance aligned to the eight Gatsby Benchmarks. PotentialU helps careers leads, heads of computing, and principals achieve outstanding results in three critical benchmarks:

Gatsby Benchmark 4: Linking Curriculum Learning to Careers

Every module is explicitly tied to real engineering job descriptions, emerging tech stacks, and tangible employment outcomes.

Gatsby Benchmark 5: Encounters with Employers and Employees

Students participate in our monthly live "Beyond the Classroom" talks, hearing directly from working software architects, engineers, and tech founders.

Gatsby Benchmark 6: Experiences of Workplaces

Through our BridgED employer brief system, learners execute genuine industry briefs, receiving structured feedback and verifiable portfolio evidence.
